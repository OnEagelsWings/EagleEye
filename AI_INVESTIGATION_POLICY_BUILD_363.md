# AI Investigation Policy — Build 363

- Explicit GO remains required before autonomous research.
- After GO, AI may plan/delegate bounded research waves, monitor crawler/connector jobs, fuse evidence, form review-required hypotheses and prepare the written dossier.
- Dossier must disclose object-store and team-search validation state.
- Backend degradation may reduce research completeness; it must never be hidden from the lead investigator.
- AI has no direct network, shell, credential or backend-admin authority.
- Hypotheses remain human-review-required and are never promoted to facts automatically.
