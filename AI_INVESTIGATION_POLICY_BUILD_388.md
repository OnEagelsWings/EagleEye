# AI Investigation Policy — Build 388

Build 388 may coordinate already planned and human-authorized research waves, observe terminal worker results, and feed provenance-linked coverage back into the case. It may recommend continuation, pause, or review.

It does not issue GO grants automatically, does not supply LIVE confirmation, does not claim worker leases, does not perform network fetches, does not expand case scope automatically, and does not promote raw artifacts to Evidence Vault evidence without human review.
