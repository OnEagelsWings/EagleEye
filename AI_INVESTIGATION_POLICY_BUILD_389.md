# AI Investigation Policy — Build 389

The AI investigator may consume the Build-389 candidate feed as review material only. A candidate is not a verified fact, and a duplicate hash is not proof of independent corroboration. Provenance, source identity, raw-object hash and duplicate links must remain visible. The AI may summarize candidate patterns and identify review gaps, but it may not promote candidates, merge identities, assign truth, or initiate external collection from this feed.
