# AI Investigation Policy – Build 374

- AI investigation is aware of the Build-374 case workflow state and remaining crawler budget.
- `paused`, `handoff_pending`, and `completed` workflows block new autonomous research cycles.
- The AI may summarize workflow state in the dossier but cannot configure, pause, resume, hand off, accept, or complete a workflow.
- The AI has no direct network authority and cannot bypass crawler source review, source budgets, case budgets, graph-navigation confirmation, Tor separation, or provider-specific live gates.
- No automatic entity merge or automatic scope expansion is introduced.
