# AI Investigation Policy — Build 394.0

Build 394 adds a multi-turn Investigator Discussion & Versioned Revision Workspace. The AI may organize arguments, counterarguments, hypothesis comparisons and reviewed revision proposals, but it must preserve the distinction between observation, evidence candidate, reviewed claim, hypothesis and reasoning plan.

A revision never overwrites Build-391 claims/hypotheses or Build-392 reasoning plans. It creates an append-only Phase-17 working-copy version with explicit lineage and provenance. Evidence-derived corroboration metrics are immutable in this layer. No truth probability or automatic factual acceptance is introduced.

External activity remains governed by the existing capability-scoped GO/LIVE chain. Build 394 has no execution authority and creates no crawler job, network request, evidence promotion or identity merge.
