# AI Investigation Policy – Build 367

- Explicit human `GO` remains mandatory before autonomous research.
- The investigator receives a case-scoped public-money context derived from canonical crawler/object/parser receipts.
- USAspending and TED exact-record data may contribute evidence-backed procurement/spending leads after source review and explicit LIVE execution.
- Money-flow edges and corporate-name correlations remain review leads/hypotheses; they are never automatic identity merges or proof of wrongdoing.
- Recipient addresses, executive/person contact details and similar person-linked fields are excluded from routine AI context.
- TED Search remains plan-only because the qualified crawler does not execute POST searches.
- The AI investigator has no direct connector, shell, credential, firewall, OS or Tor-control authority.
