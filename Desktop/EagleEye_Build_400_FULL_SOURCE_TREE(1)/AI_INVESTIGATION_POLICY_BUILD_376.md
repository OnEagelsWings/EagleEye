# Build 376 – AI Investigation Policy

The AI investigator may incorporate source coverage, bounded absence observations, staleness and open research gaps into a review dossier. It must not interpret coverage as truth probability, must not treat an absence observation as proof of non-existence, and must not promote absence to counterevidence without separate analyst reasoning and evidence. Research gaps do not grant network authority. Any future crawl still passes the existing case workflow, source review, budget, OPSEC and explicit human confirmation gates.
