# AI Investigation Policy — Build 396

New Evidence Candidates, finalized Corroboration Reviews and reviewed Claims are change signals, not facts. The AI may identify active Claims, Hypotheses and Reasoning Plans that should be re-analysed and may create review proposals. It may not change the active case state, adopt a version, promote evidence, issue GO/LIVE, merge identities, or execute external collection.

A re-analysis branch requires analyst review and the exact `CREATE REANALYSIS BRANCH` confirmation. Branch creation does not adopt the branch. Active-state adoption remains governed by Build 395.

No truth probability or automatic truth acceptance is introduced.
