# AI Investigation Policy — Build 378

- The AI may display recent Build-378 voice intent state in the dossier.
- Voice/transcript interpretation is operator-editable and is not evidence.
- Voice has no direct crawler, transport or network authority.
- Voice cannot auto-select sources, expand case scope, approve providers, invoke Tor, merge identities or confirm facts.
- Any edit to transcript or source selection requires a new intent and fresh human confirmation.
- Confirmed crawler work must pass the current case-workflow/source/budget/backpressure gates and is delegated to the governed queue.
