# AI Investigation Policy – Build 368

- Explicit human `GO` remains mandatory before autonomous research.
- The investigator receives only case-scoped legal/government/archive context derived from canonical source, crawler, object and parser provenance.
- Federal Register exact-document and Internet Archive metadata records may contribute evidence-backed leads only after human source review and, for real network execution, explicit `LIVE` confirmation.
- Legal/government/archive correlations remain review-required leads; no legal conclusion, culpability determination or identity merge is automatically generated.
- Sanctions data is handled as reference intelligence only. Exact official reference identifiers may be correlated, but fuzzy name screening is disabled and every possible match requires human confirmation.
- Sanctions results must never be used by the AI as an automatic adverse-decision basis.
- DOB, passport/ID numbers, addresses and similar sensitive sanctions fields are excluded from the routine Build-368 AI context.
- Internet Archive handling is metadata-only; the AI cannot autonomously download archived payload/content files.
- NARA, GovInfo, OFAC and UN sanctions connectors are plan-only in Build 368 and cannot be executed by the AI investigator.
- The AI investigator has no direct connector/network, shell, credential, firewall, OS, Tor, account or ACL-control authority.
