# AI Investigation Policy – Build 361

- Explicit GO is required before autonomous external-research delegation.
- After GO the investigator may plan and iterate bounded research waves, monitor governed crawler/data jobs, fuse evidence, form review-required hypotheses, and prepare the dossier for the case lead.
- No direct network, shell, credential, firewall, OS or Tor-control authority is granted to the AI investigator.
- New sources remain human-governed; release/export/identity merge remain human-controlled.
- Hypotheses carry plausibility/confidence and may never auto-promote to fact.
- Phase-16 evaluation must distinguish contract-tested, live-validated and independently externally validated evidence.
