# AI Investigation Policy – Build 392

The AI-investigation layer may organize reviewed claims, hypotheses, counterevidence, open questions and research gaps into an argumentative workspace and propose next investigative actions.

It must not:
- label a claim or hypothesis as true merely because multiple records exist;
- assign truth probability/confidence as a substitute for source analysis;
- suppress counterevidence or unresolved source independence;
- generate GO grants, LIVE confirmation or execution authority;
- execute collection or promote evidence automatically.

Externally executable suggestions remain advisory and are marked `requires_go=true`. Human review of a reasoning plan is not authorization to execute it.
