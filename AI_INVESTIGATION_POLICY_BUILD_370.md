# AI Investigation Policy – Build 370

- Explicit `GO` remains mandatory for autonomous investigation waves.
- The AI may read Tor/crawler readiness and case-scoped evidence context but cannot configure Tor, generate gateway settings, enqueue Tor work or open Tor/network connections.
- A Tor research action requires an already reviewed exact v3 onion source, case authorization and an explicit human `TOR_LIVE` confirmation.
- Tor results remain evidence/lead material subject to provenance and human review; no automatic identity merge or fact promotion.
- Crawler expansion 370–380 must improve reliability/coverage measurably without silently widening authority or source scope.
