# AI Investigation Policy – Build 365

- Explicit `GO` remains mandatory before autonomous research.
- After GO the investigator may plan/delegate bounded research waves, monitor crawler/connectors, fuse evidence, form review-required hypotheses and produce a written evidence-first dossier for the lead investigator.
- Build 365 adds a **read-only operational preflight** before a research wave. Critical/high-attention operational incidents can place the AI cycle into `operations_hold` before any new wave is started.
- The AI dossier records local operational readiness, incident count and worker health, while continuing to disclose missing external validation.
- Cross-case default-deny remains a hard context boundary. Operational metrics and traces for another case must not enter investigator context.
- The AI does **not** gain direct queue-recovery, network, shell, credential, firewall, OS, ACL or Tor-control authority.
- Human lead review remains mandatory for hypotheses, dossier release and recovery decisions.
