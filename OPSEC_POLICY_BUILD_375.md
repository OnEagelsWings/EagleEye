# OPSEC Policy – Build 375

Build 375 adds AI-plan boundary monitoring. OPSEC may identify and cancel affected-case queued jobs carrying invalid Build-375 AI-plan tags. Running-worker force kill remains disabled. No firewall, OS, Tor, credential, account or ACL mutation is granted. Provider-specific LIVE/TOR_LIVE gates remain separate and cannot be converted into generic AI execution authority.
