# AI Investigation Policy – Build 364

- Explicit `GO` remains mandatory before autonomous research.
- After GO the investigator may plan/delegate bounded research waves, monitor crawler/connectors, fuse evidence, form review-required hypotheses and produce a written evidence-first dossier for the lead investigator.
- Build 364 adds remote-team awareness: the dossier records whether remote mode is configured and whether remote deployment is externally validated.
- Cross-case default-deny is a hard context boundary; data from an unassigned case must never be included in the investigator context.
- Infrastructure degradation or missing external validation must be disclosed in the dossier.
- No direct network, shell, credential, firewall, OS or Tor-control authority is granted to the AI investigator.
