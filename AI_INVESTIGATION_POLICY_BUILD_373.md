# AI Investigation Policy – Build 373

- AI may consume aggregate case-scoped Analyst Graph state and review backlog context.
- Graph comparison weights and source-quality scores are not identity probabilities.
- AI cannot approve entity links, merge entities, select graph pivot sources, or execute graph navigation.
- Graph navigation requires an authenticated analyst, already-evidenced approved sources and exact `NAVIGATE` confirmation.
- AI cannot expand research scope autonomously from graph adjacency.
- Dossiers must preserve facts, hypotheses, counterevidence, uncertainties and review-required graph edges separately.
