# AI Investigation Policy – Build 371

- Explicit GO remains mandatory for autonomous investigation research.
- Entity Resolution v2 scores are evidence weights for review prioritisation; they are not identity probabilities.
- The AI may report candidate links, contradictions, common-name risk and independent-source support.
- The AI may not confirm identity, approve a same-entity link, create a destructive merge or bypass independent human review.
- Crawler-to-entity leads remain leads until reviewed; their source/crawl/fetch/object provenance must be shown in the dossier context.
- Strong identifier conflicts remain veto evidence and cannot be overridden by fuzzy/name similarity.
- Cross-case entity context is prohibited.
