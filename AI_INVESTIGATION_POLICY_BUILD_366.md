# AI Investigation Policy – Build 366

- Explicit human `GO` remains mandatory before autonomous investigation cycles.
- Corporate-data acquisition is not direct AI network authority. The AI may use reviewed corporate evidence already present in the case and may reason over the resulting case-scoped corporate summary.
- Build 366 adds receipt awareness and safe organization context to the evidence-first dossier.
- Routine AI context excludes address fields, EINs and person-linked fields from the corporate normalized record projection.
- Corporate relationship/correlation output is a lead/hypothesis and cannot auto-merge identities or promote a hypothesis to fact.
- Missing external validation, replay/fixture provenance and source-review state must remain visible.
- The AI cannot widen source allowlists, bypass `LIVE`, approve a source, store/use connector credentials, mutate system controls, or release/export evidence without the existing human gates.
