# AI Investigation Policy – Build 372

- Entity-resolution scores remain prioritisation weights, not identity probabilities.
- The AI may read Build-372 holdout/calibration status and case-scoped crawler source-quality bands for dossier context.
- The AI may not alter Entity Resolution v2 thresholds automatically from evaluation results.
- The AI may not label a person/entity as confirmed solely from a review-positive classification or a high source-quality score.
- Crawler source quality is an evidence-quality signal only and cannot bypass independent human review.
- The AI has no automatic entity-merge authority, no merge-approval authority and no direct network authority added by Build 372.
- Synthetic holdout results are not represented as external real-world validation.
