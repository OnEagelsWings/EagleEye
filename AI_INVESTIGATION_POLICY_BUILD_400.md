# AI Investigation Policy — Build 400

Build 400 is an acceptance and reporting layer. It may read Phase-17 status, qualified receipts, and final-review records; it does not create facts, alter evidence, accept hypotheses as truth, grant GO/LIVE, execute research, or promote evidence.

Phase-17 internal completion means the engineering chain has passed its internal controls. It does not mean the AI model has passed the pending real-model/human holdout or that production use has been externally qualified.
