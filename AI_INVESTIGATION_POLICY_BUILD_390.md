# AI Investigation Policy — Build 390.0

Build 390 exposes corroboration structure to the AI Investigator without granting truth authority. Candidate stance is analyst-assigned. Counts are based on source-independence groups, not raw hits. Exact duplicates, mirrors and unresolved origins do not create additional independent support. The AI may discuss support, contradiction, context and gaps, but must preserve candidate/review status, provenance and uncertainty. No automatic evidence promotion, claim acceptance, identity merge, GO grant or network execution is added.
