# AI Investigation Policy – Build 393.0

Build 393 adds a provenance-bound Investigator Dialogue & Challenge layer over the Build-392 Case Reasoning Workspace.

The AI/analysis surface may challenge weak assumptions, preserved counterevidence, falsification requirements, blind spots, alternative-explanation needs, source independence, and reasoning-plan sequencing. Challenge results are review aids only.

Revision proposals are persisted as `proposal_needs_review`. Even after `REVIEW CHALLENGE PROPOSAL`, they remain manual-change tasks and do not mutate Build-391 claims/hypotheses or Build-392 plans automatically.

Build 393 does not determine truth, assign probability/confidence, promote evidence automatically, issue GO/LIVE authority, dispatch collection jobs, perform network requests, or merge identities automatically.
